    Our story

    Built in Manicaland.
    Driven by trust.

    TENDA has carried Manicaland home for decades — over Christmas Pass, through the Eastern Highlands and into Harare's ranks. The buses changed; the promise didn't: leave on time, arrive safe, treat every passenger like family.

    History, dates and fleet details to be confirmed with the Mungwari family before launch.

    1. c. 1980First TENDA bus on the Harare–Mutare road — gold stripes down the side.
    2. 1980s–90sBlue-and-white "T" livery becomes a fixture of the eastern corridor.
    3. TodayNew 70-seat coaches, WiFi on board, and booking from your phone.
