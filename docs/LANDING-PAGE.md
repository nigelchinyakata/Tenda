# Landing Page

*Drafted: October 2026. Generated from DEFINE.md, Product Identity, and Magic Moment.*

## Summary

A Try-Before-Signup Hero (Lovable's pattern, in OpenTable's travel form): the route search works in the hero with no account, over a documentary photo, with the family WhatsApp message beside it as proof. Eleven sections. The hero promises a guaranteed seat home at the rank price, and one CTA, "Book my seat", repeats in the header, hero and final block.

## The Magic Moment we're promising

> When Rudo adds her mother as family contact and, on the morning of travel, her mother receives "Rudo's bus left Roadport at 07:05. Expected at Mutare rank 10:40. — TENDA", both people in the trip feel looked after.

**Hero promise:** "Going home shouldn't be a gamble"
**Primary CTA:** "Book my seat"
**Sticky CTA:** "Book my seat"

## The page

### Header

- **Logo:** the real TENDA Buses lockup (TENDA plus BUSES, without the script line), left. White on the navy hero, navy once the header turns white on scroll. Links home.
- **Nav:** How it works · Parcels · Prices · Help (4 items)
- **CTA (sticky):** "Book my seat". A blue pill, top right, that stays visible on scroll and jumps to the hero search.
- **What this proves:** this is TENDA's own site (the logo she already knows from the bus), and the price is one tap away.
- **Drop-off risk:** she thinks it's a fan page or a scam copy. Mitigation: the real logo, the tendabuses.com domain, and the verified WhatsApp number in the footer.

### Hero

- **Goal:** answer "can I book my seat home, and what does it cost?" in 5 seconds, and let her start booking without an account (Tactics #3, #5, #6).
- **Headline:** "Going home shouldn't be a gamble" (6 words, 33 characters. It is the Identity's everyday line, and names the pain she feels at the rank.)
- **Sub-headline:** "Book your seat to Mutare on your phone. Same price as the rank, US$10." (Names the route and price; 14 words.)
- **CTA:** "Book my seat". The search card's button, which opens Departures (onboarding Screen 2).
- **Visual direction:**
  - The navy header band, with a rounded bottom edge, over a documentary photo. A traveller with grocery bags at Roadport in early light, cropped so the headline sits on clean navy, never on the busy part of the photo.
  - A white search card overlaps the band: From Harare (Roadport), To Mutare, a 4-day date strip, passengers, and the blue button.
  - On desktop, a phone to the right shows the family WhatsApp message.
- **Above-fold social proof:** a trust line under the button: "Your seat, or your money back plus the next bus free · No booking fee". One real review fragment from a Facebook bus group, quoted as written: "Organized and clean… very reliable and safe." *[Get the reviewer's permission and name before publishing; until then, show it as "A passenger, Zimbabwe Long Distance Buses group".]*
- **What this proves:** she can book a real seat right now, from her phone, for the rank price.
- **Drop-off risk:** a slow page on costly data. Mitigation: one font; the hero photo under 120 KB as WebP; no video; everything below the fold lazy-loaded.
- **Reference:** Tactics #3 (10-word headline), #4 (specific sub-headline), #5 (first-person CTA), #6 (hero demonstration: the working search), #8 (testimonial fragment); decision tree pattern #1.

### Social Proof Bar

- **Goal:** no customer logos fit a bus company; this slot becomes three heritage facts in a strip just under the hero.
- **Logos:** [none yet — omit this section]. In their place, a strip of three facts, each confirmed with TENDA first:
  - "Carrying Manicaland home since [YEAR]"
  - "Leaves from Roadport (Harare)"
  - "[N] departures a day"
- **What this proves:** TENDA is the company she already knows, not a new start-up.
- **Drop-off risk:** an unconfirmed fact reads as false. Show only the facts TENDA has confirmed.
- **Reference:** Tactic #7 (adapted: facts, not logos)

### Problem

- **Goal:** name the rank-day gamble in her own words before showing the fix.
- **Headline:** "You know the rank-day gamble"
- **Body:**
  - "You queue at 06:00 and get on at 08:30."
  - "You hand a parcel to a conductor and hope."
  - "Your mother waits at the rank, not knowing which bus you're on."
- **Visual direction:** three short lines on white, each with a Phosphor line icon (clock, package, user). A second documentary photo sits beside them: a mother waiting at the Mutare rank.
- **What this proves:** TENDA understands her trip, the exact hours and worries.
- **Drop-off risk:** reads as complaining about rival buses. Mitigation: it names her experience, never a competitor.
- **Reference:** Tactic #9

### Solution / How It Works

- **Goal:** let her picture the whole booking in three steps.
- **Step 1:** "Choose your seat" + "Pick the departure and your own seat. Each bus sells only its real seats." + the seat map fragment (seat 14A in blue)
- **Step 2:** "Pay with EcoCash or cash" + "Pay US$10 with EcoCash, or hold your seat and pay cash at a TENDA office." + the payment choice fragment
- **Step 3:** "Your ticket arrives on WhatsApp" + "Your ticket comes from TENDA's verified number. Your family gets a message when the bus leaves." + the WhatsApp ticket fragment
- **What this proves:** booking takes under 2 minutes and works with what she already has.
- **Drop-off risk:** EcoCash fear. Mitigation: the cash option sits in step 2, not hidden in an FAQ.
- **Reference:** Tactic #10. Visual model: Zipline's three photo cards with a phone in step 1 ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7b0ca7a7-caf2-42a6-b315-53b86c8cd190)).

### Features as Benefits

- **Goal:** turn the mechanism into four outcomes she cares about.
- **Benefit 1:** "Your mother knows you're coming" + "Add one family contact. They get a WhatsApp when your bus leaves Roadport and when it reaches Mutare rank." + Phosphor `users` (fill)
- **Benefit 2:** "Parcels with proof" + "Get a parcel reference at drop-off. Your family gets a collection code, and only they can collect." + Phosphor `package` (fill)
- **Benefit 3:** "Christmas at the normal fare" + "Book by 15 December and you pay the normal fare, even in the festive rush." + Phosphor `calendar-check` (fill)
- **Benefit 4:** "Shift changed? Change date" + "Change your date once for free, up to 18:00 the day before you travel." + Phosphor `arrows-clockwise` (fill)
- **What this proves:** TENDA looks after both people in the trip, not just the seat.
- **Drop-off risk:** too much to read on a phone. Mitigation: 4 blocks in a 2×2 grid, each with a headline of 5 words or fewer.
- **Reference:** Tactic #11

### Pricing

- **Plans:** one fare, no tiers, as DEFINE.md sets out:
  - Seat, Harare → Mutare: **US$10**, "Same as the rank. No booking fee." Includes 1 bag plus 1 hand item, a WhatsApp ticket, and messages to your family contact. Pay with EcoCash, or hold and pay cash by 18:00 the day before. CTA: "Book my seat".
  - Parcels: small (up to 2 kg) **US$5**, medium (up to 10 kg) **US$7**, large (up to 25 kg) **US$10**. Pay at drop-off or at collection. Secondary link: "Send parcel".
  - The guarantee sits under both: "Your seat, or your money back plus the next bus free. Same-day parcels, or your fee back."
- **Annual/monthly toggle:** none. She pays per trip.
- **What this proves:** the price is the rank price, with nothing added.
- **Drop-off risk:** she assumes online costs more. Mitigation: "Same as the rank" sits next to the US$10.
- **Reference:** Tactic #14 (single-price variant), Principle 9

### FAQ

- **Q1:** "What if the bus is full when I get there?" — A: "Your booked seat is held for you. If it isn't there, you get your money back plus the next bus free."
- **Q2:** "What if I pay with EcoCash and the money goes but no ticket comes?" — A: "Your ticket comes from TENDA's verified WhatsApp number within 2 minutes. If it doesn't, call the TENDA office and quote your EcoCash reference. [Confirm the office phone number.]"
- **Q3:** "Can I pay cash?" — A: "Yes. Hold your seat online and pay at a TENDA office by 18:00 the day before. Unpaid seats are released."
- **Q4:** "My shift changed. Can I change the date?" — A: "Yes, once for free, up to 18:00 the day before you travel. Do it from your WhatsApp ticket."
- **Q5:** "Who can collect my parcel?" — A: "Only the person with the 4-digit collection code and their ID."
- **Q6:** "Does my mother need a smartphone?" — A: "No. If she doesn't use WhatsApp, the message arrives by SMS."
- **What this proves:** the scary "what ifs" all have plain answers.
- **Drop-off risk:** long answers. Mitigation: each answer is 1–2 sentences of 15 words or fewer, in collapsed rows.
- **Reference:** Tactic #15

### Final CTA

- **Headline:** "Your seat home, booked before you leave work"
- **CTA:** "Book my seat"
- **Low-friction alternative:** "Book on WhatsApp" (the backup booking line from DEFINE.md's mechanism). *[Confirm the WhatsApp number.]*
- **What this proves:** she can do it now, or the way she already knows.
- **Drop-off risk:** not ready this month. Mitigation: the WhatsApp line saves TENDA in her chats for next time.
- **Reference:** Tactic #16

### Footer

- **Compliance badges:** none held; none shown.
- **Status page:** none. The monthly on-time board takes its place. Links: "On-time record", "Contact a TENDA office", "Roadport (Harare) and Mutare rank: how to find us".

- **Reference:** Tactic #17

## Anti-patterns avoided

- **The Stock Illustration Hero:** the hero is the working search plus a documentary photo. AI-made images on the canvas are layout stand-ins only (see Images below).
- **The Multi-CTA Hero:** one blue button, "Book my seat". "Send parcel" and "Book on WhatsApp" are text links further down.
- **The Hidden Pricing:** US$10 appears in the hero sub-headline and in a pricing section linked from the nav.
- **The Hero Video That Plays on Load:** no video, because data is costly.
- **The "Trusted By" Section With No Logos:** no logo bar; heritage facts instead.
- **The Form Wall:** no account, email or ID to book.

## Refresh cadence

Recommended next refresh: 45 days from launch. First A/B test: the hero headline. "Going home shouldn't be a gamble" against "Your seat home, same price as the rank". Refresh trigger: the hero "Book my seat" click-through drops below 8% for 2 weeks.
