### 2.2 Audience [INFERENCE]

- **Core audience:** people travelling between Harare and Mutare and the Eastern Highlands, such as commuters, students, traders, and families going home ("Wasu", people from Manicaland). Evidence for this is the route focus, the regional-pride language, and the local-language comments.
- **Secondary audience:** the **diaspora** and cross-border travellers (Johannesburg), plus people sending **parcels** home.
- **Engaged fans:** a large **bus-spotter community** (Chiwambu Transit Eye, "Zimbabwe Long Distance Buses" group, African Highway Diaries). They are effectively TENDA's unpaid media team, and the brand should give them something to celebrate.
- **Device reality:** given that booking happens over WhatsApp and Facebook, the audience is very likely **mobile-first, data-cost-sensitive, and used to booking in chat**.

### 2.3 Positioning [INFERENCE]

TENDA currently holds a **heritage and regional-pride** position: "The Pride of Manicaland", "Mutare's finest", "the legend", "never broke down". It does not compete on price or on digital convenience. Competitors are now moving onto digital convenience. CAG Travellers runs pre-booking express coaches and an online booking site, `cagtours.co.zw`. The gap for TENDA is to **keep the legacy and add modern booking convenience.**

**Assessment of the attached logo [INFERENCE]:**
- **Strengths:** it is legible at distance, the forward slant suggests speed, it has a strong regional tagline, and it already uses navy and blue.
- **Weaknesses:**
  1. There is **no symbol or monogram**, so nothing works as an app icon, favicon, WhatsApp avatar or the side of a bus at small size.
  2. It is a **three-tier lockup with three type styles** (extended sans, spaced caps, script), which falls apart below about 120px wide.
  3. The script tagline won't reproduce well in vinyl livery, embroidery or a one-colour stamp.
  4. Navy and blue alone are shared with BlaBlaCar and with many generic logistics brands.
  5. The reported heritage cues (gold stripe, "T" livery, Elephant) aren't used at all.

### 3.3 Patterns worth adopting

1. **Search-first hero:** From / To / Date / Passengers, with a swap button. The widget overlaps the hero image (Klook, BlaBlaCar, KAYAK).
2. **Popular routes grid** with "from US$" and duration (Klook, Trainline).
3. **Departure cards** with a timeline, seat class and badges such as "Express" or "Last seats" (BlaBlaCar, Shopee).
4. **A three-up trust strip** directly under search (Klook).
5. **An owned illustration motif** woven into the logo world (Trainline's heart-tick).
6. **A big-wordmark footer** (Sweatpals).
7. **A chat booking channel** shown as a first-class option (Eagle Liner's WhatsApp booking). This matters especially for Zimbabwe.

## 4. Recommended direction (applied in `/website`)

- **Palette:** Tenda Navy `#0A2550`, Highland Blue `#1668D0`, Heritage Gold `#F2B233`, Mist `#EEF3FA`, Ink `#0B1424`.
- **Type:** *Archivo* (expanded, black italic) for display, which echoes the existing slanted wordmark. *Inter* for UI text. Drop the script from small sizes and keep it only for large hero or livery moments.
- **Motif:** the **"Pass line"**, a gold stroke that climbs like the road over Christmas Pass. It is used as an underline, a route timeline and a divider.
- **Voice:** warm, proud and plain-spoken ("Home is a ride away"), with some ChiShona where it is natural (e.g. *Tenda*, *Wasu*). Check every phrase with native speakers.

## 5. Logo-concept prompts

Each prompt works on its own and can go into an image model (Midjourney, Ideogram, Firefly, Freepik, Higgsfield, etc.). Image models are bad at lettering, so treat the letterforms as **sketches for a designer to redraw in vector**. Do not use the output as final artwork.

**Shared constraints (add to every prompt if your tool supports long prompts):**
> Flat vector logo on a plain white background, no gradients, no 3D, no mock-up, no photo, no bus clip-art. Colours limited to deep navy #0A2550, highland blue #1668D0 and heritage gold #F2B233. Must remain recognisable at 32×32 px and in one colour. Clean geometric construction, generous negative space, professional transport-brand quality comparable to Trainline, FlixBus and BlaBlaCar.

### Concept 1 — "The Pass" (monogram + landscape)
> Logo for TENDA, a heritage intercity bus company from Mutare, Zimbabwe, founded around 1980, nicknamed "The Pride of Manicaland". Design a bold monogram letter "T" whose crossbar becomes a mountain ridge silhouette of the Eastern Highlands, with a single gold road line climbing through the ridge like the Christmas Pass highway between Harare and Mutare. The vertical stem of the T is slightly forward-slanted to suggest motion. Pair the symbol with the wordmark "TENDA" in a heavy, extended, forward-italic grotesk, tight letter-spacing, navy. Flat vector, white background, navy #0A2550 symbol, gold #F2B233 road line, minimal, iconic, works as an app icon.

### Concept 2 — "Golden Stripe" (heritage livery mark)
> Logo for TENDA intercity coaches, Zimbabwe. Inspired by the company's reported first bus, which carried golden stripes, and its later blue-and-white livery that formed a giant letter "T" on the bus side. Create an abstract mark of three parallel speed stripes (navy, blue, gold) that sweep forward and bend at a right angle to form a stylised "T". The negative space between the stripes reads as a road. Wordmark "TENDA" in an extra-bold, wide, slanted sans-serif, with "SINCE 1980" in small, widely tracked caps beneath. Flat vector, white background, colours #0A2550, #1668D0, #F2B233, confident, retro-modern 1980s transport heritage, clean and timeless.

### Concept 3 — "The Elephant" (character mark)
> Minimal geometric elephant-head emblem for TENDA, a Zimbabwean bus company affectionately nicknamed "The Elephant" for its size and endurance. The elephant's ear forms a forward-pointing chevron and its trunk curves down into a lowercase-free capital "T" shape. Use only circles and straight lines, single-weight strokes, and solid navy #0A2550 fill with one gold #F2B233 accent (tusk or eye). It must feel strong, dependable and friendly, not cartoonish, and not like a safari or tourism logo. Place the wordmark "TENDA" to the right in a heavy, wide, italic grotesk. Flat vector, white background, scalable to favicon size.

### Concept 4 — "Route Pin T" (digital-first)
> App-icon-first logo for TENDA, a Harare–Mutare intercity bus brand modernising with online booking. A rounded-square icon in deep navy #0A2550 containing a bold white letter "T" whose stem ends in a map-pin point, with a short gold #F2B233 dash route running through the crossbar from left to right, suggesting a journey from A to B. Geometric, friendly, 8px corner radius proportion, high contrast, instantly legible at 24 px. Show it beside the wordmark "TENDA" in a heavy, extended, slightly italic sans with the "A" crossbar replaced by a small gold dash. Flat vector on white, no gradients.

### Concept 5 — "Pride of Manicaland" (badge / seal for livery and uniforms)
> Circular heritage badge for TENDA buses, Zimbabwe. Outer ring text: "TENDA • THE PRIDE OF MANICALAND •". Centre: a stylised forward-slanted "T" monogram over three layered mountain ridges (the Eastern Highlands) with a gold sun or headlight disc rising behind. Inside the ring at the bottom: "EST. 1980". Two-colour: navy #0A2550 and gold #F2B233 on white. Bold, embroidered-patch-ready, crisp vector lines, thick strokes, no thin details, works stitched on a driver's uniform and as a 60 cm decal on the rear of a coach.

### Concept 6 — "Wordmark refinement" (evolution, not revolution)
> Refine an existing bus-company wordmark "TENDA": heavy, wide, forward-slanted sans-serif capitals in deep navy. Keep the existing character but make it ownable. Cut a single gold #F2B233 diagonal speed-slash through the crossbar of the "T" and echo it in the counter of the "A". Equalise stroke weights, tighten spacing, and open the counters for small-size legibility. Below it, "BUSES" in bright blue #1668D0, geometric caps with wide tracking. Remove the script tagline from the core lockup. Show horizontal and stacked lockups plus a stand-alone "T" with the gold slash as the icon. Flat vector, white background, professional transport identity.

**Prompt tips:** Generate 4–8 variations per concept, then shortlist two directions and test them:
- as a 32px favicon
- as a WhatsApp avatar
- in one-colour vinyl on a white coach
- next to the FlixBus, Intercape, CAG and Eagle Liner logos

Then hand the winning direction to a designer for vector redrawing and a trademark search.
