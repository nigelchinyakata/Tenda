# TENDA Premium — Paper asset prompts

These prompts are for building the custom assets in Paper. The asset slots and the palette match the TENDA Premium Redesign canvas.

**How to use them.** Paste prompt 0 once at the start of the Paper session. Then run each asset prompt in order, attaching the supplied TENDA logo wherever a prompt mentions the logo.

---

## 0 · Brand context (paste first, once)

> You are designing assets for **TENDA Buses**, a family-run intercity coach company from Mutare, Zimbabwe. Its tagline is **"The Pride of Manicaland"** and its home route is **Harare ⇄ Mutare over Christmas Pass**, through the Eastern Highlands. We are repositioning TENDA as the **premium** way to travel the eastern corridor, without losing its heritage warmth.
>
> **Logo:** use the attached TENDA logo exactly as supplied. It has a heavy, extended, forward-slanted wordmark "TENDA", widely tracked "BUSES" below it, and a script tagline. Never redraw, stretch, recolour outside the palette, outline or add effects to it.
>
> **Palette (use only these):**
> - Midnight `#06152F` (primary dark ground)
> - Tenda Navy `#0B2B5C` (logo navy)
> - Signal Blue `#1570C9` (logo blue, used sparingly)
> - Brass `#C8A24B` (premium accent; highlights only)
> - Ivory `#F6F2EA` (light ground)
> - Stone `#E6E0D3` (light surface)
>
> **Type:**
> - Archivo Expanded Black Italic for headlines (it echoes the wordmark)
> - Instrument Sans for UI and body text
> - Instrument Serif Italic for the tagline and story lines
>
> **Icons:** Lucide only, 1.75px stroke, round caps.
>
> **Signature motif, "the Pass line":** layered mountain ridges in tonal navies, with a single **dotted brass road line** climbing left to right through them.
>
> **Mood:** calm, confident, cinematic and uncluttered, like a premium airline rather than a bus station. Keep large negative space and a restrained palette.
>
> **Never use:** gradients washes, glows, lens flares, neon, emoji, clip-art buses, stock-photo smiles, or text baked into images unless a prompt asks for it.

## 1 · Homepage hero illustration — "The Pass at dawn"

**Slot:** Homepage hero background. **Size:** 2880 × 1440 px (export 1440 × 720 @2x), plus a 1170 × 1300 mobile crop.

> Create a wide, flat-vector landscape illustration of the Eastern Highlands of Zimbabwe at pre-dawn, for a premium coach brand's website hero.
> - **Ridges:** three to four overlapping mountain ridges built from simple geometric polygons, in tonal navies only (`#0B2B5C`, `#0E3670`, `#08204A`) on a Midnight `#06152F` sky.
> - **Sun:** a solid Brass `#C8A24B` disc sits high on the right third, with two hair-thin concentric Brass rings at 15% and 10% opacity.
> - **Road:** a single dotted Brass road line (round dots, generous gaps) climbs from the bottom-left corner, sweeps across the lower ridges and disappears over the far-right pass, like the Christmas Pass highway.
> - **Coach:** add one tiny, simplified coach silhouette in Ivory `#F6F2EA` with two warm headlight dots, about 1.5% of the image width, on the road near the centre-left.
> - **Composition:** leave the top-left 55% calm and empty, because headline text sits there. Keep the bottom 20% low-contrast, because a booking card overlaps it.
> - **Exclude:** stars, clouds, gradients, texture and text.

## 2 · Classic coach — exterior hero photo / render

**Slot:** "Classic" class card image. **Size:** 1600 × 1200 px.

> Create a photorealistic 3/4 front view of a modern 70-seat intercity coach in TENDA livery, driving on a two-lane tarred highway through green Manicaland hills in late-afternoon light.
> - **Body:** white.
> - **Livery:** a bold Tenda Navy `#0B2B5C` sweep along the lower half that rises into a giant forward-slanted letter "T" shape on the side. Add a thin Brass `#C8A24B` pinstripe tracing the sweep.
> - **Logo:** the attached TENDA logo in navy on the upper side panel, and in white on the navy rear.
> - **Look:** clean and freshly washed, crisp reflections, shallow depth of field, cinematic 35mm look, slightly cool shadows with warm highlights.
> - **Exclude:** passengers, other vehicles, roadside signage text, and invented sponsor logos.
> - **Composition:** leave clear sky in the top third.

## 3 · Premier cabin — interior

**Slot:** "Premier" class card image. **Size:** 1600 × 1200 px.

> Create a photorealistic interior of a premium coach cabin at golden hour, seen from the aisle looking forward.
> - **Seating:** 2+1 seating with wide reclining leather seats in Midnight navy `#06152F`, with headrest covers embroidered with a small Brass `#C8A24B` "T" monogram.
> - **Details:** a warm brass reading-light trim, USB ports in the armrests, and soft ivory `#F6F2EA` ceiling panels.
> - **Window:** large windows showing blurred Eastern Highlands hills in warm low sun.
> - **Mood:** calm, quiet and uncluttered, like a business-class airline cabin.
> - **Exclude:** people, visible text other than the monogram, and TV screens showing content.
> - **Composition:** keep the lower-left area darker so a badge can sit over it.

## 4 · Route postcard — Harare → Mutare via Christmas Pass

**Slot:** Routes card 1. **Size:** 1200 × 1500 px (4:5).

> Create a vertical, editorial travel-poster illustration in flat vector with subtle grain, showing the Christmas Pass road descending towards Mutare at dawn.
> - **Road:** switchback curves through layered navy ridges (`#0B2B5C`, `#0E3670`, `#08204A`).
> - **Lights:** the valley town lights of Mutare appear as small Brass `#C8A24B` dots far below, and a Brass sun sits just above the horizon.
> - **Coach:** one small Ivory coach with headlights rounds the first bend.
> - **Composition:** keep the top 25% as plain Midnight `#06152F` sky for overlay text.
> - **Exclude:** text, people and gradients.
> - **Consistency:** match the style of the homepage hero so the set feels like one family.

## 7 · App icon / favicon — "Slanted T + Pass line"

**Slot:** App icon, favicon, WhatsApp/Facebook avatar. **Size:** 1024 × 1024 vector, plus 512, 180, 32 and 16 px exports.

> Design a single-glyph app icon for TENDA.
> - **Tile:** a rounded square (corner radius ≈ 23% of the side) filled with Midnight `#06152F`.
> - **Letter:** centred, a heavy, forward-slanted capital "T" in Ivory `#F6F2EA`. Its angle and weight must match the "T" of the attached TENDA wordmark: sample the slant from the logo; don't invent a new letterform.
> - **Road line:** a single Brass `#C8A24B` stroke (about 5% of the icon width, round caps) sweeps from lower-left to upper-right behind the T's stem, crossing it like a road climbing a pass.
> - **Exclude:** gradients and shadows.
> - **Test:** it must read clearly at 16 px. Produce a one-colour Ivory version and a one-colour Navy version.
> - **Avatar variant:** a circular crop for WhatsApp and Facebook.

## 8 · Pass-line pattern (brand texture)

**Slot:** Ticket backgrounds, packaging, uniforms, section dividers. **Size:** seamless tile, 800 × 400 px.

> Create a seamless horizontal repeat pattern of minimal line-art mountain ridges.
> - **Ridges:** a single continuous 2px Tenda Navy `#0B2B5C` stroke forming peaks of varying height.
> - **Road:** paired with one dotted Brass `#C8A24B` road line weaving beneath the ridges.
> - **Colourways:** three of them:
>   (a) navy and brass on Ivory `#F6F2EA`
>   (b) brass and Signal Blue `#1570C9` on Midnight `#06152F`
>   (c) a single-colour Stone `#E6E0D3` on Ivory for watermarks
> - **Edges:** the left and right edges must join seamlessly.
> - **Exclude:** fills and text.

## 9 · Coach livery side elevation (design spec)

**Slot:** Brand board, fleet page. **Size:** 3200 × 1000 px flat side view.

> Create a flat vector side elevation of a 12-metre, 70-seat intercity coach showing the proposed TENDA Premium livery.
> - **Base:** an Ivory `#F6F2EA` body.
> - **Sweep:** a Midnight `#06152F` sweep starts low at the front wheel arch, rises diagonally, and fills the rear third, forming the vertical of a giant forward-slanted "T". The T's crossbar runs along the window line as a navy band.
> - **Pinstripe:** a 40mm Brass `#C8A24B` pinstripe traces the leading edge of the sweep.
> - **Logo:** the attached logo (navy version) on the ivory front-side panel.
> - **Rear:** "The Pride of Manicaland" in Instrument Serif Italic Brass on the navy rear panel.
> - **Glass:** windows in dark tinted glass.
> - **Labels:** include a small Premier variant label in Brass beside the door.
> - **Exclude:** sponsor logos and phone numbers (leave a labelled box saying [CONTACT]).

## 12 · Parcels & charter spot illustrations (set of 3)

**Slot:** Service cards. **Size:** 800 × 800 px each, transparent background.

> Create three matching spot illustrations in flat vector line art, 2px Tenda Navy `#0B2B5C` strokes with single Brass `#C8A24B` accents, on a transparent background.
> - **(1) Parcels:** a neatly taped parcel with a luggage tag shaped like a ticket.
> - **(2) Charter:** a coach front view with a small ribbon bow on the grille, for weddings and celebrations.
> - **(3) Cross-border:** a coach crossing a simple truss bridge.
> - **Style:** Lucide-compatible: round caps, no fills except the Brass accents, and a consistent 2px stroke at 800px.
