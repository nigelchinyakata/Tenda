# Magic Moment

*Drafted: October 2026. Generated from DEFINE.md.*

This is a **committed hypothesis to test**, not a proven fact. Confirm or replace it within 4–6 weeks of launch using real retention data.

## The recommended magic moment

> When Rudo adds her mother as family contact and, on the morning of travel, her mother receives the WhatsApp "Rudo's bus left Roadport at 07:05. Expected at Mutare rank 10:40. — TENDA", both people in the trip feel looked after. That's the moment Rudo knows TENDA is worth booking every month. It happens in WhatsApp, on the family contact's phone, set up during the booking on the website.

**Where it sits:** Set up in the first session (the family contact is added on the "Your details" screen); delivered on the day of travel, usually 1–7 days after booking.

**Time-to-aha target:** The family contact receives the departure message within 10 minutes of the bus leaving Roadport. The setup (adding the contact) takes under 30 seconds in the first session.

**Success metric:**
- 60% of paid bookings include a family contact.
- 95% of departure messages are sent within 10 minutes of departure.
- Leading retention signal: first-time bookers whose family contact received a departure message rebook within 45 days at a higher rate than those without one. Target 40% vs a baseline to be measured.

## Why this is the primary

Getting a ticket on WhatsApp is becoming the Zimbabwean standard (Inter Africa Express and Intercape already sell over WhatsApp), so it proves the booking worked but doesn't make TENDA special. The departure message to the family is TENDA's worldview made real, "every trip home has two people in it", and no Zimbabwean operator does it. It also follows a well-documented pattern: Uber puts Share My Trip at the centre of its safety promise, and WhatsApp's own aha is the two-way confirmation. Candidate 1 is its required setup step: it must work every time for this moment to happen.

## Three candidates considered

### Candidate 1: The ticket lands on WhatsApp

- **Action:** Pays US$10 with EcoCash for seat 14A.
- **Wow:** Within a minute, a ticket with her name, seat and departure time arrives from TENDA's verified WhatsApp number. "It's real, and I didn't queue."
- **Where:** First session.
- **Time-to-aha:** <60 seconds from EcoCash approval to the WhatsApp ticket; <5 minutes from landing on the site.
- **Comparable evidence:**
  - Inter Africa Express (Zimbabwe) sells tickets on WhatsApp: "Skip the queues and book your… ticket directly on WhatsApp".
  - Intercape runs an official WhatsApp booking line.
  - Calendly's aha is the first meeting booked without back-and-forth.
- **Risk:**
  - Competitors already offer it, so it's parity, not delight.
  - A slow or failed EcoCash callback turns the aha into anxiety.
- **Metric:** 90% of paid bookings receive the WhatsApp ticket within 60 seconds of payment; under 2% of bookings need a manual ticket resend.

### Candidate 2: "Rudo's bus left Roadport at 07:05" (primary)

- **Action:** Adds her mother as family contact while booking.
- **Wow:** On travel morning her mother gets the departure message and knows exactly when to walk to Mutare rank. Both people in the trip feel looked after.
- **Where:** Set up in the first session; delivered on the day of travel.
- **Time-to-aha:** Contact added in <30 seconds; message delivered within 10 minutes of departure.
- **Comparable evidence:**
  - Uber Share My Trip lets "friends and family easily see your trip status".
  - WhatsApp's aha is the first message sent and replied to.
  - Facebook's aha is connecting with people you know (7 friends in 10 days): value grows when the people who matter are in it.
- **Risk:**
  - It only happens if a family contact is added; if adding it feels like extra admin, uptake stays low.
  - It depends on staff marking departures promptly; a late or wrong message damages trust.
  - The family contact must consent and can opt out ("Reply STOP").
- **Metric:** 60% of paid bookings include a family contact; 95% of departure messages are sent within 10 minutes of departure; family-contact bookings rebook within 45 days at a higher rate than bookings without one.

### Candidate 3: Walking past the queue to a held seat

- **Action:** Shows her ticket at Roadport at 06:40 and boards.
- **Wow:** Seat 14A is waiting while others queue. "I got an extra hour of sleep." The guarantee is kept.
- **Where:** Day of travel.
- **Time-to-aha:** Boarding within 5 minutes of arriving at Roadport.
- **Comparable evidence:**
  - Airline mobile boarding passes (show the phone, walk on).
  - Uber's aha is the first ride completed within minutes of requesting it.
  - Superhuman's aha is the promised result delivered in the first session.
- **Risk:**
  - A single seat not held breaks trust publicly, so the guarantee must be watertight and staff trained.
  - It happens days after booking, so it's slow to reach.
- **Metric:** 99% of booked seats honoured at boarding; 40% of first-time bookers book again within 45 days.
