Name

TENDA, written "TENDA Buses" online. The booking service is simply "TENDA".

  • Say it: two syllables, easy in English and Shona.
  • Spell it: five letters, one way to write it.
  • Search it: clashes with the Tenda router brand. Pair with "Buses" or "Mutare" online.
  • Feel it: decades of Manicaland goodwill.
  • Domain: tendabuses.com (primary), booktenda.com redirecting. Check .co.zw with a Zimbabwean registrar.

Worldview

Every trip home has two people in it: the one on the bus and the one waiting at the other end.

Everyday line: "Going home shouldn't be a gamble."

Contrarian Belief

Most bus companies sell a seat to whoever is standing at the rank. We believe a seat should belong to a person before they leave home, and that the family waiting in Mutare deserves to know where she is.

Tone of Voice

  • Warm, but not soppy: a trusted older cousin who knows the road.
  • Plain, but not cold: short sentences; numbers, not adjectives.
  • Proud, but not boastful: decades of Manicaland, said quietly.
  • Certain, but not pushy: facts, never fake urgency.

Language: English with a little Shona in greetings and sign-offs ("Mhoro", "Famba zvakanaka"). Never in instructions, prices or the guarantee.

We say: your seat, home, your family, on time, leaves at, here's what happens next, sorry, your money back.

We don't say: luxury, world-class, class is permanent, your comfort is our priority, valued customer, Dear Sir/Madam, ALL CAPS, multiple exclamation marks, unexplained jargon.

"Rudo, your seat 14A is held. The bus leaves Roadport at 07:00 tomorrow. We'll tell your mother when you're on the road. Sleep well."

Visual Style

Lane: documentary photography of real Manicaland journeys and real people, under the existing TENDA logo.

  • Early-morning light on the Harare–Mutare road and Christmas Pass, warm and honest.
  • Real people, never models. Credited bus-spotter photos of TENDA coaches, with permission.
  • Logo navy and blue as anchors; clean white screens with one clear action each.
  • One person or one bus per image, generous sky and road.
  • Pair a photo with a phone showing the WhatsApp ticket.
  • Headlines on clean navy or white, never over busy photo areas.

References: Trainline (clarity), Humans of New York (one person, one line of story), Airbnb host photography (real people, real places).
