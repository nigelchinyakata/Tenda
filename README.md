# TENDA Buses — brand redesign

Step one of the TENDA brand redesign: research plus a website prototype.

- `docs/tenda-brand-research.md`: TENDA's business, audience, positioning and identity, with verified, reported and inferred findings kept apart. Also covers the competitor benchmark (Mobbin + Firecrawl), opportunities, and six logo-concept prompts.
- `website/`: a static homepage prototype (no build step). Open `website/index.html` in a browser, or run `npx http-server website`.

All routes, times, fares and contact details in the prototype are **placeholders** until TENDA confirms them (see §6 of the research doc).
